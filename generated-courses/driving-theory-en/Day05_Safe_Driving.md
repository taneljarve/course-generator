# Day 5: Safe Driving Principles

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this day, you will:
- Apply defensive driving techniques
- Handle emergencies
- Understand driver fatigue

## Key Concepts

### Defensive Driving

- Anticipate other drivers' mistakes
- Maintain safe space around your vehicle
- Stay alert and focused
- Adjust to conditions

### Managing Emergencies

**Brake Failure**
- Downshift to slow down
- Use parking brake as last resort
- Steer to safe area

**Steering Failure**
- Grip firmly
- Reduce speed gradually
- Signal and use hazard lights

**Tyre Failure**
- Hold steering firmly
- Don't brake suddenly
- Slow down gradually

### Driver Fatigue

Signs of fatigue:
- Yawning frequently
- Difficulty focusing
- Wandering thoughts

Prevention:
- Take breaks every 2 hours
- Don't drive after long work hours
- Stay hydrated

## Practical Exercise

1. Study emergency procedures
2. Plan long journeys with breaks
3. Practice concentration techniques
