# Day 1: Traffic Signs and Markings

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this day, you will be able to:
- Identify the main categories of traffic signs
- Understand the meaning of warning signs
- Recognize regulatory and informational signs

## Key Concepts

### Sign Categories

Traffic signs are divided into three main categories:

**Warning Signs (Triangular)**
- Yellow triangle with red border
- Warn of potential hazards ahead
- Examples: slippery road, children crossing, sharp curve

**Regulatory Signs (Circular)**
- Red circle: Prohibitions (stop, no entry, speed limits)
- Blue circle: Mandatory actions (turn left, turn right, roundabout)

**Information Signs (Rectangular)**
- Green: Motorway directions
- Blue: Service information
- Brown: Tourist attractions

## Guided Explanation

### Understanding Warning Signs

Warning signs alert drivers to dangers. When you see these signs, slow down and be prepared to react:

- **Slippery Road** (slippery when wet) - Reduce speed and avoid sudden movements
- **Children Ahead** - Watch for children crossing
- **Sharp Curve** - Slow down before the curve, not inside it
- **Steep Hill** - Use a lower gear on steep inclines

### Understanding Regulatory Signs

These signs are legally enforceable:

- **Stop** - Come to a complete halt
- **Give Way** - Yield to other traffic
- **Speed Limit** - Do not exceed the stated speed
- **No Entry** - Do not enter this road

### Road Markings

Road markings provide additional guidance:

- **White solid line** - Do not cross
- **White dashed line** - Safe to pass
- **Yellow line** - No passing zone
- **Stop line** - Where to stop at intersection

## Practical Exercise

1. Study 10 different traffic signs from each category
2. Match each sign to its meaning
3. Practice identifying signs while on foot in traffic
