# Day 4: Intersections and Turning

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this day, you will:
- Turn safely at intersections
- Understand turning rules and signals
- Navigate different intersection types

## Key Concepts

### Before Turning

1. Check mirrors
2. Use indicator in time (at least 30 meters before)
3. Move to correct lane if required
4. Slow down

### Turning Types

**Left Turn**
- Watch for oncoming traffic
- Stay in left lane
- Watch for pedestrians

**Right Turn**
- Stay in right lane
- Watch for cyclists
- Watch for pedestrians on crosswalk

### Complex Intersections

- Multi-lane intersections
- Roundabouts with multiple exits
- Signal-controlled turning lanes

## Practical Exercise

1. Practice signaling before turns
2. Study intersection layouts
3. Watch experienced drivers turn
