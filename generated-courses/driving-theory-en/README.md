# Driving Theory Course

This comprehensive course covers the fundamental theory needed to become a safe and responsible driver.

## Course Overview

| Day | Topic |
|-----|-------|
| 1 | Traffic Signs and Markings |
| 2 | Right of Way and Priority Rules |
| 3 | Speed and Distance Management |
| 4 | Intersections and Turning |
| 5 | Safe Driving Principles |

## Who Is This Course For?

This course is designed for:
-Learners preparing for their driving test
-New drivers wanting to refresh their knowledge
-Anyone interested in understanding traffic rules

## How to Use This Course

Each day covers theory followed by practical exercises. Progress through one day at a time for best results.
