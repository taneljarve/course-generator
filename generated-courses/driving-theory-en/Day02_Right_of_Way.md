# Day 2: Right of Way and Priority Rules

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this day, you will:
- Understand who has priority at intersections
- Know when to yield to other road users
- Understand priority at roundabouts

## Key Concepts

### Priority at Intersections

**Uncontrolled Intersection**
- Yield to vehicles coming from your right
- If in doubt, wait

**Traffic Light Intersection**
- Green: Proceed
- Yellow: Stop if safe to do so
- Red: Stop completely

**With Stop Sign**
- Come to complete stop
- Yield to all traffic
- Proceed when safe

### Priority Rules

- Emergency vehicles have priority when using sirens
- Pedestrians at crossings have priority
- Trolley buses and trams have priority in their lanes

### Roundabout Rules

- Yield to traffic already in the roundabout
- Use your indicator when exiting
- Keep to the left, exit to the left

## Practical Exercise

1. Identify different intersection types
2. Practice yield rules at each type
3. Study roundabout entry and exit rules

## Reflection Questions

1. Why is it important to yield?
2. What happens if you don't yield at a roundabout?
