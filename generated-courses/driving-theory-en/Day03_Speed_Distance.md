# Day 3: Speed and Distance Management

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this day, you will:
- Calculate safe following distance
- Adjust speed to conditions
- Understand stopping distances

## Key Concepts

### Two-Second Rule

Keep at least two seconds behind the vehicle in front:

1. Note when the vehicle passes a fixed point
2. Count "one thousand one, one thousand two"
3. If you reach the point before "two", you're too close

### Stopping Distance Formula

Stopping distance = Thinking distance + Braking distance

- At 50 km/h: thinking ~9m + braking ~14m = 23m total
- At 80 km/h: thinking ~14m + braking ~35m = 49m total

### Speed and Conditions

Adjust your speed when:
- Weather is poor (rain, fog, ice)
- Road surface is damaged
- Visibility is low
- Traffic is heavy

## Practical Exercise

1. Practice the two-second rule while a passenger
2. Calculate stopping distances at different speeds
3. Identify when conditions require slower speed

## Reflection Questions

1. Why does stopping distance increase with speed?
2. How does weather affect braking distance?
