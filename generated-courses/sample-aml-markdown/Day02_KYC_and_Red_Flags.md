# Day 2: KYC and Red Flags

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the role of KYC in AML programs
- Identify a few common red flags in account behavior
- Practice turning observations into review notes

## Key Concepts

- Know Your Customer (KYC)
- Customer profile
- Expected behavior
- Red flags

## Guided Explanation

KYC is the process of understanding who the customer is, what they are using the product for, and what normal account behavior should look like.

Red flags do not automatically prove wrongdoing. They are signals that tell an analyst to slow down, compare the activity with the customer profile, and ask better questions.

Examples of red flags can include:

- sudden spikes in activity
- frequent transfers to unrelated parties
- behavior that does not match the stated purpose of the account

## Practical Exercise
